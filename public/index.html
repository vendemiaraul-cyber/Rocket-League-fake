PLAY ONLINE

Get your friends on the pitch

Matches are online by default: anyone who opens the game sees the open matches and can jump in. Here's how a game night goes.

1

Host a match

Open the game, tap MULTIPLAYER, then HOST SERVER. Your match appears in everyone's server list right away.

2

Friends join

They open the same game and tap your match, or open your invite link. Up to six players, and a bot fills the empty spot so the teams stay even.

3

Kick off

The host starts the match, or after 30 seconds everyone votes. Two minutes on the clock, and anyone sitting out watches from the spectator box.

INVITE LINKS

WHAT'S INSIDE

Made for quick, loud matches

Boost, jump and flip

Grab boost pads, fly into aerials and flip into the ball for a power shot.

Huge goal explosions

Every goal gets a slow-motion blast, fireworks and the scorer's name on the big screen.

Chat and stickers

Talk in the lobby and during the match, and drop stickers that pop up over your car.

Fair play built in

A swear filter, warnings and reports keep the chat friendly, and a movement check catches cheaters.

Switch tabs, keep playing

The match runs on the server, so it never pauses. If you step away, your car leaves the field until you're back, or the host can turn on an autopilot.

Practice against the bot

No one around? Play the bot anytime to warm up your aerials.
